What This Practises

Questions cover grammar, punctuation, word classes, sentence structure, verb forms, clauses, Standard English and vocabulary. Years 3 & 4 focus on core word classes and basic punctuation; Years 5 & 6 add clauses, active/passive voice and more complex punctuation; Year 6 SATs Practice draws on the trickier concepts typically tested in the SATs grammar paper. Every question shows a full explanation after answering, not just whether it was right or wrong.
