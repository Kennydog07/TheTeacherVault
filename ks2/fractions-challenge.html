What This Practises

Topics adjust to the year level selected: Year 3 focuses on recognising fractions, fractions of amounts and simple comparisons; Year 4 adds equivalent fractions, ordering and same-denominator addition; Year 5 and Year 6 add subtracting, mixed numbers, improper fractions and calculations across different denominators. Every answer is worked out from scratch and simplified where appropriate, with a worked explanation shown after every question.
