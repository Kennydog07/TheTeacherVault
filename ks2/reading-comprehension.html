For parents & teachers

How Marking Works Here

Multiple-choice questions have one clearly correct answer and are marked automatically, with a full explanation shown either way. Short-response questions cannot be marked automatically -- instead, pupils compare their own answer against a model answer and the supporting evidence, encouraging them to check their own reasoning rather than chase a single "correct" wording. The Reading Skills Summary only shows a percentage for skills that were tested with objectively-marked multiple-choice questions during that session.
