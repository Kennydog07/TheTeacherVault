    What this practises

    Quick-fire recall of multiplication and division facts for the 2 to 12 times tables — the same fluency the Year 4 Multiplication Tables Check and KS2 SATs arithmetic paper both rely on. The Year group buttons are a starting suggestion for which tables to focus on, not an exact statutory boundary — every child progresses at a different pace, so feel free to mix and match tables.
