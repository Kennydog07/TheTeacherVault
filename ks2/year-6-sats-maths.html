      What this practises

      Arithmetic practice covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, percentages, order of operations and missing-number problems. Reasoning practice covers multi-step problems, fractions, ratio, percentages, measurement, time, money, geometry, statistics and number reasoning — the same style of skills assessed across the KS2 SATs arithmetic and reasoning papers. Every question is generated freshly using original templates, not reproduced from any past paper.
