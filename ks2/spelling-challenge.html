    About the word lists

    The "Year 3/4" and "Year 5/6" lists here are the statutory National Curriculum in England spelling lists (English programmes of study, Key Stage 2, Appendix 1) — the exact 100 words per list that schools are required to teach, reproduced in full. The "Extra Practice" list is additional vocabulary that is not part of the statutory requirement, included for further practice. The app always shows which list a word belongs to, so it's never mistaken for a curriculum requirement.
