Questions about paying

How do I pay?

All payments go through Gumroad, which runs The Teacher Vault store. Gumroad takes the payment securely and emails you your receipt and download. We never see your card details. You can read more in our privacy policy.

Why does Gumroad show a price in US dollars?

Gumroad may show prices in US dollars. The prices on this page are in pounds, so the amount you pay can differ slightly depending on the exchange rate and your card provider.

Can my school pay by invoice?

Yes, for school licences. Email us at theteachervault@gmail.com with your school's name and roughly how many staff will use the tools, and we'll reply with a quote and an invoice.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. Every Pro tool has a free version that opens straight in your browser with no account. Try it, and upgrade only if you want the extra features.

What is your refund policy?

Please see our refund policy: Refund policy.

Will the free tools stay free?

Yes. The free tools, student apps, Quick Notes and printables are free to use, with no sign-up.
