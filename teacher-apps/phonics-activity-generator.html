Phonics Activity Generator

Create a quick phonics activity for your group.

How to use

Choose a level, target sound and activity type, then click Generate.

  • "Mixed sounds" picks a different grapheme each time you generate — useful for quick review.
  • Real or Alien? always mixes in invented words, whatever the alien words setting — that's the point of the activity.
  • Some graphemes (like "oo" in "moon" vs "book", or "ow" in "cow" vs "snow") can sound different depending on the word — worth a quick check with pupils before you start.
  • Alien words are original, pronounceable made-up words — never copied from any official phonics screening check.
