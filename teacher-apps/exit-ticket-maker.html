Exit Ticket Maker

Create a set of print-ready exit tickets for the end of a lesson in seconds - pick a format, add today's topic, and print.

Create your exit tickets

How to use

Pick a format, type in today's topic (and choose a Key Stage if you'd like the wording adjusted), choose how many tickets you need per page, then select Generate tickets.

  • 3-2-1 — pupils note 3 things they learned, 2 questions they still have, and 1 thing to explore further.
  • Traffic light self-assessment — pupils self-assess their confidence against today's learning and explain why.
  • One thing / One question — a quick, low-effort reflection for the end of a busy lesson.
  • Quick check — type in your own recap question and pupils answer it in the space provided.
  • Rate & explain — pupils rate their understanding from 1-5 and explain their rating in one sentence.
  • Two stars and a wish — two things that went well today, and one thing to improve.

Use Print for a cut-ready sheet sized to the number of tickets you chose, or Copy as text to paste the ticket wording elsewhere, such as a slide.
