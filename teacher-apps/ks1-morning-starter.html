KS1 Morning Starter Generator

A ready-to-use start-of-day activity in seconds.

How to use

Choose a year group, subject and difficulty, then click Generate Morning Starter for an instant set of questions.

  • "Mixed KS1" blends Year 1 and Year 2 level questions; "Mixed" subject blends English and maths.
  • Tick Include a challenge question for one extra stretch question at the end.
  • Tick Include answers to show the answer key straight away, or reveal it later with Show Answers.
  • Use Regenerate for a fresh set with the same settings, Copy Questions to paste into a slide, or Print for an ink-friendly worksheet.

Ready when you are

Choose your settings above and generate a morning starter for the board, a slide or a worksheet.
