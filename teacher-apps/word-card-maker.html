How to make keyring word cards
  • Pick the age, a topic and a card shape. You get 10 words for that topic straight away.
  • Press Shuffle words for a different 10, or tap any word to type your own.
  • A matching picture is added for you. Choose a different one or change the colour if you like.
  • Press Print A4 sheet. Set the scale to 100% (or “Actual size”) so the cards come out at keyring size. The rounded card is about the size of a bank card.
  • For a sturdy keyring, laminate the sheet, cut out each card leaving a small clear edge, and punch the circle at the hole mark.
  • Thread the cards onto a 25 to 30 mm binder ring or keyring so children can flip through them during writing.
  • Tick “Print a back” to add a name label. Print the second page on the back of the sheet, flipping on the long edge.
