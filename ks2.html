Which Tool Should I Use?

Preparing for SATs week

Start with SATs Practice

Year 6 SATs Maths Practice and Year 6 SATs English Revision are built around the actual paper structure -- the most direct way to prepare for the real thing.

Building number confidence

Try Arithmetic Trainer or Fractions Challenge

Both adjust to the exact year level selected. Add Times Tables Challenge for quick daily fluency practice.

Grammar or spelling gaps

Try SPaG Challenge or Spelling Challenge

SPaG Challenge covers grammar and punctuation; Spelling Challenge focuses purely on the statutory word lists.

Reading comprehension

Try Reading Comprehension or Inference Detective

Reading Comprehension Practice covers all seven reading skills across 30+ texts; Inference Detective is a detective-themed deep dive into inference specifically.

Improving writing

Try Sentence Improver or the Creative Writing Generator

Sentence Improver strengthens existing sentences step by step; the Creative Writing Generator sparks brand new story ideas to write from scratch.

About These KS2 Resources

Every tool on this page is free, runs entirely in the browser, and needs no account or login. Questions are generated freshly each time rather than pulled from a fixed bank, so pupils don't just memorise answers. Where a tool references the National Curriculum — such as the statutory Year 3/4 and Year 5/6 spelling lists — the words are reproduced exactly as published, and clearly labelled so they're never mistaken for something else. None of these tools collect personal information; any saved progress (like a best streak or a list of tricky spelling words) stays on the device it was created on.
