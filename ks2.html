About These KS2 Resources

Every tool on this page is free, runs entirely in the browser, and needs no account or login. Questions are generated freshly each time rather than pulled from a fixed bank, so pupils don't just memorise answers. Where a tool references the National Curriculum — such as the statutory Year 3/4 and Year 5/6 spelling lists — the words are reproduced exactly as published, and clearly labelled so they're never mistaken for something else. None of these tools collect personal information; any saved progress (like a best streak or a list of tricky spelling words) stays on the device it was created on.
