Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.

Are the free apps really free?

Yes. Every free app — teacher tools, student apps and Quick Notes — opens or downloads directly from this site at £0, with no Gumroad step, no hidden trial period and no forced upgrade. Only Pro upgrades are purchased separately through Gumroad.

Do I need to create an account?

The apps themselves generally do not require an account, although Gumroad may ask for basic information (like an email address) to process the download or purchase.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Free apps open directly in a modern browser — nothing to install. Pro purchases are a single file from Gumroad that also opens straight in your browser.

Will they work on Windows and Mac?

Browser-based HTML apps should work on modern browsers on both Windows and macOS. If a specific app has different requirements, this will be noted on its product page.

Do they work offline?

Standalone apps that contain all required content can work offline once downloaded. Check the individual app page or Gumroad listing to confirm before relying on offline access.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free versions provide the core tool at no cost, opening straight in your browser. Pro versions add additional functionality — such as more advanced options or features suited to regular use — and are sold separately as their own product through Gumroad.

Is there a subscription?

No. Current Pro mini apps are individual, one-off purchases unless a product listing specifically states otherwise.

Where are downloads hosted?

Teacher tools and their Pro upgrades are handled securely through Gumroad — the Teacher Vault website is the discovery and information hub, and Gumroad delivers the actual files. Student apps and Quick Notes study guides are the exception: they open or download directly from this website, with no Gumroad step.

Are student apps free?

Yes. Teacher Vault student apps marked Free are free to download and use, with no subscription required.

Can schools use them?

Individual product licences and terms apply and can vary by app. Please check the licensing information on the relevant Gumroad product page before using an app across a school, department or multiple devices, and get in touch if you need clarification.
