Privacy Policy

Overview

The Teacher Vault website is a discovery and information site for a library of mini apps. It is built as a simple static website, and does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from visitors.

Information collected by this website

This website does not use a login system, database, or server-side backend. Browsing the site does not require you to provide any personal information. The contact form on this site opens your own email application rather than submitting data to a server.

Third-party services

Pro purchases are handled by Gumroad, a third-party platform; free apps open or download directly from this site. When you purchase a Pro app through Gumroad, Gumroad may collect and process information such as your email address and payment details, in order to fulfil your order. This is governed by Gumroad's own privacy policy and terms, which we encourage you to read on their website.

Analytics

Basic website analytics (such as anonymised visit counts) may be added to this site in the future to help understand how it's used. If and when analytics are added, this page will be updated to reflect that.

Cookies

This website does not currently set its own cookies. Third-party services linked from this site, such as Gumroad, may use their own cookies in line with their respective policies.

Children's privacy

Some revision apps are intended for use by school-age students. This website itself does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any download or purchase through Gumroad is subject to Gumroad's own policies.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated from time to time as the site and its products evolve.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
