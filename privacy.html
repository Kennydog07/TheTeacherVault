Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version

  • You can use this website without an account and without giving us any personal information.
  • This website does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Anything you type into our free in-browser tools stays in your own browser. It is not sent to us.
  • Paid Pro products are sold through Gumroad, which handles the payment and your order details.
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless, to count visits in aggregate.

Who we are

The Teacher Vault (theteachervault.com) is a UK website offering teacher tools, student revision apps, Quick Notes, guides and printables. For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), the data controller for this website is [PLACEHOLDER: data controller's full name or business name, and postal address].

You can contact us about privacy at [PLACEHOLDER: privacy contact email].

Information this website collects

The site is a set of static pages. There is no login, no user accounts and no database of visitors. We only receive personal information if you choose to send it to us, for example by email.

Contact form and email

The form on our Contact page does not send anything to a server. When you press send, it opens your own email app with your message filled in, and you decide whether to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it and whatever you write. We use this only to reply to you, and we keep it only for as long as we need to deal with your message. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are visited, so we know what is useful. It does not use cookies or local storage, and it does not build a profile of you or follow you across other websites. Cloudflare processes basic technical information such as the page address, the referring page, your browser type and your approximate country. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Server logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify's servers process technical information such as your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages and keep the service secure. See Netlify's privacy policy.

Information stored in your browser

Some free tools save your work or settings in your own browser using a feature called local storage, so it is still there next time you open the tool on the same device. Examples include the seating plan maker, the teacher to-do board, retrieval roulette and some revision apps that remember your progress. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for theteachervault.com.

The homepage also uses session storage to remember that you have already seen its opening animation. This is cleared when you close the browser tab.

Please don't type pupils' personal or sensitive information into any tool. Use first names, initials or codes instead.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't show a cookie banner. The local and session storage described above is used only to make the tools work as you would expect.

Third-party services

Gumroad (payments)

Pro tools and other paid products are sold through Gumroad. When you buy, you leave this website and Gumroad acts as the merchant of record: it takes your payment, handles your order and deals with sales tax. Gumroad collects details such as your name, email address and payment information. We never see your full card details. Gumroad shares your name, email address and order details with us so that we can provide what you bought and help with any questions. How Gumroad uses your information is covered by Gumroad's privacy policy.

For refunds, please see our refund policy: [PLACEHOLDER: refund policy link].

Google Fonts

Our pages load a font from Google Fonts. To do this, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.

Newsletter

Our newsletter sign-up links take you to beehiiv, the service that runs our newsletter. If you subscribe, beehiiv stores your email address and sends the newsletter for us. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. See beehiiv's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

We link to other websites, such as our Pinterest and Facebook pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use their own cookies once you visit them.

Children and young people

Many of our revision apps and activities are made for pupils. You can use them without signing up or giving us any personal information. Purchases are meant for adults, such as teachers, schools and parents, and must be made through Gumroad by an adult.

If you are a young person: you don't need to tell us anything about yourself to use this site. Please don't type your full name, address or other private details into any of the tools. If you want to buy something or sign up for the newsletter, ask a parent, carer or teacher to do it for you.

Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We only share it with the service providers named on this page, where needed to run the site, or if the law requires us to.

International transfers

Some of the services named on this page are based outside the UK, for example in the United States, so information they process may be handled there. These providers publish details of the safeguards they use in their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop or limit how we use it. You also have the right to object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us at [PLACEHOLDER: privacy contact email].

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the way the site handles information changes, and change the date at the top.
