Number Bonds Challenge

What makes the number?

Find the missing number to complete each number bond — ten frames show the numbers to 10, and there's a more independent mode with no pictures too.

Choose bonds

Choose how to play

For Grown-Ups

What this practises: number bonds to 10 and 20 — knowing pairs of numbers that add up to a target, a key building block for mental addition and subtraction.

Suggested year group: Bonds to 10 suits Year 1; Bonds to 20 and the no-picture Missing Number mode suit Year 2.

How to help: encourage your child to work it out rather than guess — the ten frame picture shows the "known" part filled in, so they can count the empty spaces. Accuracy matters more than speed, so there's no pressure timer during play.

What to try next: KS1 Maths Challenge covers a wider range of number and calculation skills.
