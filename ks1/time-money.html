Time & Money Practice

Clocks, coins and everyday maths.

Practise reading clocks and working with UK coins — the kind of maths that comes up every single day.

Choose a category

Choose a year group

For Grown-Ups

What this practises: reading analogue clocks (o'clock, half past, quarter past/to and five-minute intervals) and working with UK coins — counting, comparing and simple change.

Suitable for: Years 1 and 2. Year 1 stays with o'clock and half past; Year 2 adds quarter past, quarter to and five-minute intervals.

How to help: a real clock or real coins alongside the screen make this much more concrete — try matching what your child sees here to the clock on the wall or coins from your purse.

Try next: KS1 Maths Challenge covers wider KS1 number and measurement skills. Parents may also find KS1 Maths: What Parents Need to Know useful.
