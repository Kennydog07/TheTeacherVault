For Grown-Ups

What this practises: number, calculation, shape, measurement and simple problem-solving skills across the KS1 maths curriculum.

Suggested year group: choose Year 1, Year 2, or Mixed KS1 for a blend of both.

How to help: encourage your child to work out the answer before choosing — accuracy matters more than speed at this age. If they get one wrong, look at the explanation together.
