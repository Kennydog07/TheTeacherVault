Why Teachers Choose These Plays

Every child can take part. Flexible cast lists show how to scale each play up or down, with group roles and line counts so you can match parts to readers.

No sheet music needed. Songs use traditional tunes everyone already knows, with photocopiable lyric sheets.

Curriculum links built in. Each pack links to EYFS areas of learning or the National Curriculum for England and PSHE, with discussion questions and follow-up activities.

Common Questions

Which play is best for a nativity alternative?

The Moon Who Was Scared of the Dark is a gentle, non-religious winter play for Reception to Year 2, set on the longest night of the year.

Which play works for Odd Socks Day or Anti-Bullying Week?

The Odd Sock is a 15-minute play for Reception and Year 1 about a sock who learns you don't have to match to be friends.

Which play is best for Year 6 leavers?

Lost Property includes a dedicated Year 6 leavers' ending, and Unit 7 Wants a Birthday is written for Years 5 and 6.
