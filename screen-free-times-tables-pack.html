Screen-Free Times Tables Pack

Six weeks of low-pressure times tables practice for Years 3–6, with no screens needed.

What’s included

  • 22-page printable PDF and an editable Word version (.docx)
  • Guidance for adults and six screen-free games using everyday materials

How to use it
