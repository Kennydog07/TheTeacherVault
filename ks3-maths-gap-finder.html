Curriculum

Topics Checked

  • Number: decimal place value, negatives, order of operations, fractions, FDP conversions, prime factors, HCF and LCM, ratio, percentage increase
  • Algebra: simplifying, substitution, expanding and factorising, equations with unknowns on both sides, nth term, rearranging, inequalities, y = mx + c, double brackets
  • Geometry & Measures: angle facts, area of triangles and trapeziums, polygons, circles, volume, Pythagoras’ theorem, metric units and map scales
  • Statistics & Probability: averages and range, mean from a frequency table, pie charts, probability, expected outcomes, relative frequency, listing outcomes

Matched to the KS3 National Curriculum for England. Written in British English, and every answer has been checked.

How It Works

  1. Print the check for the topic you are about to teach.
  2. Run it in 10–15 minutes at the start of the first lesson – no calculators.
  3. Mark with the answer key, noting which wrong answers pupils gave and what they suggest.
  4. Decide with the class tracker: reteach the whole class, run a small-group reteach, or move on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does each check take?

Each one-page check is designed for 10–15 minutes at the start of a topic, with no calculator needed.

Which year groups is it for?

KS3: Years 7, 8 and 9. Questions rise in difficulty, so early questions suit Year 7 and later ones stretch towards Year 9.

Are answers included?

Yes. Every check has a full answer key that also lists common wrong answers and the misconception each one suggests.

What format is the pack?
