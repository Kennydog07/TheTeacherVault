Numbers to 10

Count. Match. Play.

For Grown-Ups

What this practises: recognising numerals 0–10, counting objects accurately, matching quantities to numbers, and ordering numbers.

Suggested age range: approximately 3–5 years. Younger children may enjoy just exploring "Meet the Numbers" first.

How adults can help: count out loud together, touching each object once as you say each number. If a game feels too tricky, try "Meet the Numbers" again before coming back to it.

What to try next: once counting feels easy, Pattern Detective uses similar early reasoning skills with shapes and colours.
