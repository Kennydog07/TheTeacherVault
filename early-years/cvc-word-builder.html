CVC Word Builder

Build a word!

Great word building!

For Grown-Ups

What this practises: blending individual letter sounds together to build and spell simple, decodable three-letter words.

Suggested age range: approximately 3–5 years, especially children who are already confident spotting beginning sounds.

How adults can help: look at the picture together and say the word slowly, stretching out each sound ("c...a...t") before your child chooses the letter tiles. There's no audio in this game, so your voice does the job!

What to try next: if your child needs more practice with individual letters first, Letter & Sound Explorer is a gentler starting point.
