Pattern Detective

What comes next?

For Grown-Ups

What this practises: spotting and continuing a repeating pattern — an important early reasoning and pre-maths skill.

Suggested age range: approximately 3–5 years. Start with the easiest pattern and only move on when it feels simple.

How adults can help: point to each shape in turn and say it out loud together ("circle, square, circle, square..."). The Clue button groups the pattern to make the repeat easier to spot.

What to try next: Numbers to 10 uses similar early reasoning skills with counting and ordering.
