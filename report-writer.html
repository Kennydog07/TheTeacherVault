Report Writer

Report Writer is a free report-writing assistant that helps you turn rough notes into clear, consistent pupil reports without starting from a blank page each time. Pick a tone, use the built-in sentence starters and structure prompts, and keep every report in your own words.

Who Report Writer is for

Primary and secondary teachers writing pupil reports — whether it's one class or a whole year group. Free to use, works in your browser, and needs no account or sign-up.

Sentence starters and structure prompts for common report sections
