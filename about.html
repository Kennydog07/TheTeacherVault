About The Teacher Vault

The Teacher Vault was created around a simple idea: teachers should spend less time on repetitive admin, and students should have access to straightforward revision tools without unnecessary subscriptions or complicated software.

Small, focused tools

Rather than building one large platform that tries to do everything, The Teacher Vault provides small, focused mini apps. Each one is built to solve a specific, everyday problem — writing a report, planning a lesson, or revising a GCSE topic — and to do it well.

Saving teachers time

Teaching involves a lot of repetitive admin. The Lite tools in The Vault aim to take a small amount of that off your plate, for free, without asking you to change how you already work.

Free revision, properly

Student apps in The Teacher Vault are free to download and use. Students shouldn't need to pay a subscription, hand over ongoing payment details, or create accounts just to practise for their exams.

Useful technology, not technology for its own sake

Every app in The Vault exists because it solves a real, specific problem — not because it was possible to build. We'd rather ship one genuinely useful tool than a dozen half-finished ones.

A growing library

The Teacher Vault is intentionally small today and grows over time. New teacher tools and revision subjects are added as they're built and tested, rather than promised in advance.
